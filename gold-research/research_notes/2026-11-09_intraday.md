## 1. London open / Asian range breakout on XAUUSD

**Sources**
- https://github.com/MHZardary/london-strategy-backtest — READ IN FULL
- https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/777540 ("London Breakout on Gold: 3.7 Years of Backtests", Oct 2026) — SNIPPET

- https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/777404 ("I Tested 400+ Strategy Variants on Gold") — SNIPPET
- https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/london-breakout-strategies/ — fetch FAILED; SNIPPET only (EUR/USD test, not gold)
- https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/london-breakout-eur-usd — SNIPPET (EUR/USD)
- https://www.breakoutalerts.io/blog/asian-range-breakout-strategy, https://goldhunts.com/gold-strategies/xau-session-breakout, https://www.dailyforex.com/forex-articles/london-breakout-strategy/210474 — fetch FAILED / SNIPPET (rule descriptions only)

**Mechanical rule set (the common denominator across sources)**
- Range: high/low of the Asian session. Window is NOT standardised: 00:00–07:00 GMT/UTC is the most common (used by the GitHub backtest and most blogs); 23:00–07:00 UTC and 01:00–08:00 server-time variants also appear. Any test must fix one.
- Entry: a candle (M15 most common, M30 also) closes outside the range after the London open (07:00 or 08:00 UTC depending on BST). Stop-order-at-the-level variants also exist.
- Stop: just beyond the opposite side of the range (i.e. stop = 1× range).
- Target: 1× to 2× the range (GitHub repo default 2×; fixed-pip TP optional). Time stop: close at end of day.
- Discretionary / unstated: direction filter (trend MA, RSI/MACD), news-day skip, whether both sides are traded after a first stop-out, exact definition of "London open" across DST.
- Origin: no identifiable author. The method is a session-specific descendant of opening-range breakout; forum/blog folklore from the early-2000s forex era. No source names a populariser.

**Performance evidence**
- GitHub (MHZardary): EUR/USD, GBP/USD, GBP/JPY only, 2024-07 to 2025-07, 242 signals, 54.1% directional accuracy, average PnL +0.00018 per trade; spread/commission NOT modelled; no gold. Label: self-reported, not gold.
- MQL5 post 777540 (gold, 3.7 years real-tick data, two brokers): ~715 trades, PF 1.37–1.38, ~49% winners, max DD 17–19%; at 0.5% risk PF 1.32, DD 11.2%. Cost model not visible in snippet; author also sells a related EA. Label: self-reported.
- MQL5 post 777728 (gold 2008–2026, costs included, 288 parameter versions, in-sample/out-of-sample split): only 14/288 versions profitable in both halves vs ~15 expected by chance; best OOS versions were buy-only and lost in the first half; best win rates 44–46%. Author's conclusion: no real edge; apparent gains are gold's 2017–2026 uptrend. Label: self-reported but the most skeptical and methodologically sound retail test found.
- MQL5 post 777404 (400+ variants): breakouts lost 2016–2021, worked 2022–2026. Label: self-reported.
- EUR/USD comparators: Backtrex 2016–2026 H1 London breakout, 36.3% win rate with 2:1 bracket, total return −28%, max DD −31.4% (0.02%/side commission). Quantified Strategies' EUR/USD version "often resulted in losses" (snippet). Neither is gold.
- Verdict: **no independently substantiated gold record**; the two most careful self-reported tests disagree (PF ~1.37 vs. no edge after OOS), and the no-edge test is the one that controls for data snooping.

**Layer verdict:** Usable only as a *time-window + range-definition* layer (fixed Asian window, London-open trigger). Main caveat: the bulk of positive gold backtests are long-only beta on gold's uptrend; require two-sided, cost-inclusive, OOS test before using the entry/exit.

- https://scinapse.io/papers/2735606157 ("Overnight versus day returns in gold") — SNIPPET
- https://tradingonmt5.pages.dev/london-ny-gold-scalp.html, https://sweep-96c.pages.dev/ — SNIPPET (signal-vendor pages; London close 16:00–17:00 UTC "secondary reversal window")

**Mechanical elements that can be stated**
- COMEX ORB: range = 08:20–08:35 ET; buy stop above range high / sell stop below range low; stop at opposite side; volume filter (vendor pitch, Pineify). Discretionary: target, filter thresholds.
- 08:30 ET releases: FXStreet's recurring NFP studies (samples of 30–35 releases) measure XAU/USD reaction at 15 min / 1 h / 4 h. Findings: asymmetric — gold reacts more to weak NFP than strong (2021 version: +$3.89 avg on misses vs +$0.03 on beats); correlation with surprise is strongest at 15 min and "virtually none" at 4 h. This is a descriptive statistic, not a trading rule.
- "London close reversal": vendor pages define a 16:00–17:00 UTC window where an "excessive" London move (one page: 3+ standard deviations) at a higher-timeframe level is faded; entry after a liquidity sweep of Asian/PD high-low and close back inside within 1–3 candles; stop beyond extreme; target VWAP or M15 50 EMA. "Excessive" and "major level" are discretionary. No numbers.
- Academic time-of-day facts (futures, not CFD): Caminschi & Heaney find significant returns for informed traders in the 4 minutes after the London PM fix *starts* (15:00 London), none after publication; volume +47.8% vs prior 20-min average. A CBS thesis reports a "hat-shaped" intraday seasonality (gold rises in Eastern/Asian hours, falls the rest of the day) and drops around fix times in the alleged-manipulation years. An overnight-vs-day paper finds positive overnight / negative day returns in COMEX front-month and London fix.

**Performance evidence**
- COMEX ORB / NY reversal / London-close reversal as trading rules: **no usable performance record found** (vendor and TradingView idea posts only).
- Intraday seasonality / fix effects: **independently substantiated** (peer-reviewed JFM paper; student thesis) but these are return anomalies measured on futures/ETF, not a retail rule with costs; the fix-window effect pre-dates the 2015 LBMA electronic auction and may no longer exist.

**Layer verdict:** Usable as a *time-window* layer (08:20–08:35 ET ORB; avoid first 2–5 min of 08:30 data; 15:00 London fix as a volatility marker). Main caveat: zero cost-inclusive evidence for any of the NY entry/exit rules; the only hard evidence is academic, futures-based and partly stale.

## 3. VWAP / anchored VWAP on gold intraday

**Sources**
- https://www.forex.in.rs/brian-shannon-strategy and https://www.forex.in.rs/anchored-vwap-basics — SNIPPET (secondhand summaries of Shannon)
- https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/95585897-maximum-trading-gains-with-anchored-vwap---the-perfect-combination-of-pr — SNIPPET (book exists; AVWAP for entries/exits/stops)
- https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/777386 ("Anchor VWAP to Session Opens: DAX40 & Gold") — fetch FAILED; SNIPPET
- https://blog.traderspost.io/article/using-vwap-for-gold-trading-strategies — fetch FAILED; SNIPPET
- https://in.tradingview.com/script/oSV81CXs-VWAP-SD2-Reversion-Long/ — SNIPPET (FX majors, not gold)

**Mechanical elements**
- Shannon-style AVWAP (stocks-oriented; no gold-specific rules published by Shannon): anchor at a significant event (swing low/high, gap, earnings → for gold: session open, prior-day high/low, NFP candle); trend bias = price above rising AVWAP; entry = first pullback to AVWAP that holds (one summary: retrace ≤38.2% of the opening leg); stop = below the structural higher low (some summaries: 1 ATR below AVWAP); scale 1/3 at 1R, stop to breakeven. Discretionary: choice of anchor, "holds".
- Session-anchored VWAP for gold (MQL5 post): anchor at 13:30 GMT NYSE open (or COMEX open), trade in direction of price vs. AVWAP after the first 15–30 min.
- Standard VWAP mean reversion: enter when price closes beyond ±2 SD band, with RSI/MACD confirmation; target = VWAP; stop beyond band. The published script is tuned on 5-min FX majors, not gold; author warns the small target is "sensitive to execution costs".
- TradersPost: anchors at yearly low / monthly pivot / prior-week high-low; claims 1–2% intraday extensions from AVWAP "often precede reversion" — no numbers.
- Implementation note: on spot gold CFDs there is no true volume; TradingView uses tick volume, so VWAP on XAUUSD is an approximation (one script note: `hlc3` source is closest).

**Performance evidence:** **No usable performance record found** for any VWAP/AVWAP rule on gold (no backtest with costs; Shannon's own evidence is anecdotal stock charts).

**Layer verdict:** Usable as a *bias/entry-location* layer (direction = side of session-anchored VWAP; pullback-to-AVWAP entries; ±2 SD as stretch filter). Main caveat: no volume on CFD gold and no cost-inclusive test; treat AVWAP as a reference line, not a tested edge.

## 4. Supply/demand zones, order blocks, "smart money concepts"

**Sources**
- https://forums.babypips.com/t/odd-enhancers-sam-seiden/39321 and https://theshortcableguy.blogspot.com/2012/05/odds-enhancers-from-ota.html — SNIPPET (Seiden/OTA odds enhancers)
- https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/education/lessons-from-the-pros-201109070000 — SNIPPET (Seiden, OTA)
- https://cn.tradingview.com/script/yFmy3VBi-Order-Block-OB-Statistics-Real-Win-Rate-vs-Random-Levels/ — SNIPPET (placebo-controlled OB test)
- https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/order-block-gbp-usd and https://backtrex.com/fr/blog/order-block-ict-backtest-trading — SNIPPET
- https://fxnx.com/en/blog/smart-money-concepts-work-backtest-evidence — fetch FAILED; SNIPPET

**Reproducible definitions found**
- Seiden zone: a basing area (small-bodied candles) followed by a strong one-directional departure; zone = body-to-wick extremes of the base. Odds enhancers (checklist, scored): strength of the departure, distance travelled ("profit margin"), freshness (number of prior revisits; each revisit weakens the zone), how price returns to the zone, time spent at the level, time of day. OTA never published the full list; a cited threshold of "score ≥8" is one trader's recollection. Scoring remains discretionary.
- Order block (Backtrex implementation): last opposite-direction candle before an impulsive move that creates displacement; zone = candle body (not wicks); entry at 50% of the body; stop 5 pips below OB candle low; exit on structure reversal or fixed 2–3R.
- Break of structure: no quantified definition (swing lookback, threshold) exists in any source found; every definition is visual.

**Performance evidence**
- TradingView OB-statistics script (1,854 zones, 31 runs across instruments/timeframes/barrier widths): no configuration showed a reliable difference between real order blocks and matched placebo levels; pooled estimate slightly below zero, CI includes zero. Label: self-reported (open-source method, replicable).
- Backtrex GBP/USD H1 OB, Oct 2016–Oct 2026: 203 trades, 38.4% win rate, PF 0.83, −15% total, max DD ~28% (0.02%/side commission). Label: self-reported (vendor engine). Not gold.
- FXNX 1,000-setup FX test (snippet): OB alone does not hold price; only OBs with strong displacement + FVG showed value. Label: self-reported.
- Seiden method: **no usable performance record found**. No gold-specific test of any SMC rule found.

**Layer verdict:** Usable only as a *level-generation* layer if coded mechanically (impulse-candle body, freshness count). Main caveat: the only controlled test says OBs are indistinguishable from random levels; everything else is discretionary and untested on gold.

**Mechanical rules in the sources**

**Result pattern**
- GitHub: Jan 2023–Apr 2024 walk-forward, 847 trades, 77.3% win rate, PF 2.31, DD 4.2%, "18.6%/month" — spread, slippage and commission are NOT mentioned anywhere; figures are internally inconsistent (77% wins at 1.5R implies PF ≈5, not 2.31). Label: self-reported, gross of costs.
- Gold/Spread Algo v6: 2012–Jan 2026, 47,393 trades, 54.74% win rate, net +$1,040 total — roughly $0.02 per trade; listing says results are "without commissions"; with a 20–40 cent gold spread on a 5-pip (50-cent) stop, the spread alone is 40–80% of risk, so the edge is negative after costs by simple arithmetic.
- Forum M5 breakout EA: +$792, PF 1.16 over 599 trades; M3 variant negative; cost model unstated.
- MQL5 and AlgoTradingSpace posts state the general finding: strategies that look profitable with zero spread "break even or lose" at 15–25 pip ($0.15–0.25) spreads, 60 pips at news.

**Label:** self-reported throughout; the consistent pattern is positive gross / negative or ~zero net; **no cost-inclusive backtest showing a positive net edge for M1/M5 EMA/RSI scalping on gold was found**.

**Layer verdict:** Not usable as an entry layer. Caveat: with a $0.20–0.40 spread, any target under ~$2 makes costs >10–20% of reward per trade; the only salvageable element is the session filter (13:00–17:00 UTC) that the research repo adopted for spread reasons.

- Exness: Standard ~0.16–0.25 (quoted as "1.6–2.5 pips" in some sources, "16–25 pips" in others — same number, different pip definition); Raw ~0.06 + commission; Zero 0.0 + $3.50–5.50/side.
- Q1 2026 aggregator averages (London/NY overlap): Exness 6.0, IC Markets 8.5, Pepperstone 10.0 in 1-cent "pips" → $0.06–0.10 raw.

**Session pattern (third-party MT5 logs; no broker publishes a session split)**
- Asia (00:00–07:00 UTC): ~30–50% wider than London/NY; one log: $0.35 vs $0.15 at the London/NY overlap; raw-account 20–35 points vs 8–15 at overlap.
- London (07:00–12:00 UTC) and NY (12:30–17:00 UTC): tightest; overlap 13:00–16:00 UTC tightest of all.
- Daily break / rollover (5 pm ET = 22:00 UTC in winter, 21:00 UTC in summer): spreads of $1.20–2.80 for 5–20 minutes reported; raw-account 80–200+ points; one MQL5 tick study (4.83M ticks, 20 days, one broker) found substantial widening also *outside* the rollover window and warns against generalising across brokers. Many EAs block 21:55–23:05 UTC.
- News (NFP/CPI 08:30 ET): widening of $1–5 for 30–90 seconds, normalising in 2–5 minutes; market orders in the first seconds can start $0.40–0.60 underwater from spread alone (broker/EA-vendor figures, unaudited).
- Unit warning: "pip" on gold means $0.01 at some brokers and $0.10 at others; "point" is the 3rd decimal on 3-digit feeds. Always convert to USD/oz.

**Label:** contract specs = broker-published (credible, but region-specific and not fully retrieved); session/rollover/news spreads = self-reported third-party logs.

**Layer verdict:** Directly usable as the *risk/execution* layer: trade 07:00–17:00 UTC, hard no-trade 21:45–23:15 UTC and ±3 min around 08:30 ET releases (also satisfies FTMO's 2-min rule), size on $0.30 spread assumption, and model costs at ≥$0.35 round-trip on FOREX.com standard pricing. Caveat: all session figures are from unaudited logs; log your own broker's spread before fixing thresholds.

## Cross-cutting conclusion

Only three things in this survey are independently substantiated, and none is a retail rule: (a) gold's intraday seasonality / London-fix anomalies (academic, futures), (b) FTMO's statement that XAUUSD is its most traded symbol, (c) broker contract specifications. The one methodologically careful retail test found (288 variants, OOS, costs) says the gold session-breakout has no edge beyond long gold beta; the one controlled SMC test says order blocks equal random levels. The reusable layers are therefore time windows, range definitions and execution constraints, not entries or exits.
